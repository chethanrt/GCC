Sameer Shariff

Founder & CEO

Sameer Shariff is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Impelsys, guiding the organization across its global operations. As the driving force behind Impelsys’ vision and long-term strategy, he has played a pivotal role in shaping the company’s growth, culture, and market leadership. Under his entrepreneurial guidance and deep domain expertise, Impelsyshas become a trusted partner in publishing, healthcare, education, and enterprise sectors, serving more than 300 clients worldwide through its products and services.

Sameer holds a graduate degree in Economics (Entrepreneurial Management) from the Wharton School of Business, University of Pennsylvania. His thought leadership has been recognized across multiple industry platforms, with features in Forbes, India Today, and Silicon India, and invitations as a speaker at leading events. A passionate sports enthusiast, he has completed the New York and Paris Marathons. Sameer is also an active supporter of the Indian start-up ecosystem, contributing through advisory roles, operational involvement, and early-stage investments.
